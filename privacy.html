Privacy

Last updated: August 2026

What this site is

Course Map is a free course discovery tool. We list and link to courses hosted by third-party providers (Udemy, Coursera, edX, and others). We do not host course videos or sell enrollments.

Data we store on your device

  • Theme preference (light/dark) in localStorage
  • Saved courses (favorites) in localStorage
  • App shell cache via the service worker for offline use

This data stays on your device. We cannot read it from a server.

Contact form

Messages sent through the contact form are delivered via FormSubmit to the site owner’s email. Use the form only for legitimate feedback (broken links, suggestions). Do not send sensitive personal data.

Third parties

Clicking “View Course” takes you to an external provider site. Their privacy policies and terms apply there. We may use privacy-friendly analytics in the future; this page will be updated if that changes.
